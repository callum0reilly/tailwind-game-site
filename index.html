About

Tailwind: Sky Loop is a one-touch arcade flyer. Hold to climb, keep holding to loop, and let go to dive. Build speed, throw loops, thread rings and collect coins to unlock new planes, all while a storm chases you.

FAQ

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage resets it.

There's no sound. Check the 🎵 and 🔊 buttons on the main menu and your phone's media volume.

Does it need an internet connection? No. The game works fully offline.
