PRIVACY

Tailwind: Sky Loop

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

Tailwind doesn't collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, coin total, unlocked planes, selected plane, number of runs and sound settings in your device's local app storage so your progress is kept between sessions. This information never leaves your device and isn't accessible to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Information we collect

None. The app makes no network requests and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

Permissions

The app uses vibration for haptic feedback during play. It doesn't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
